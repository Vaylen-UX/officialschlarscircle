About Our Circle

More Than Just a Study Group

We started The Scholar's Circle because studying alone gets tough, but studying with the right people makes all the difference. Whether we're clearing late-night doubts, sharing notes, or pushing each other before exams, we're here to grow together.

When We Started

Created on 20/10/2026. What started as a simple group chat for friends quickly became our daily space to stay consistent and focused on our goals.

What We Believe In

"One Team, One Dream, Zero Limits." Nobody gets left behind here—when one of us is stuck on a topic, the rest of the circle steps in to help.

Group Chat Rules

How we keep the GC helpful for everyone

For All Members
01

Keep It About Studies

Use the chat for doubts, notes, exam updates, and study plans. Avoid random forwards or spam that bury important messages.

02

Help Each Other Out

No doubt is ever "too basic." Be respectful, explain concepts patiently, and back each other up without judgment.

03

Be Active, Not a Silent Reader

Join in on daily study targets, share what you're revising, and answer questions whenever you know the topic.

04

Stick to the Group Schedule

Cooperate with Zubair, Amit, and Rajdeep when study timings or group check-ins are announced.

05

Name Your Notes & PDFs

Whenever you send photos of notes or PDFs, add the subject or chapter name so everyone can find it easily before exams.

06

No Off-Topic Chat During Study Hours

When people are locked in and studying, keep the chat quiet or strictly study-related. Save casual talk for break times.
